Room access

  
Blocks the whole room. Tapping Start commits it — the sweep still
    runs, since a few rows can usually be reached anyway. One table you cannot
    get to is a skip, not this.

  

  
  

    
    
  






  
Skip table

  
Why this table went unmeasured. One tap skips the rest of
    it and moves you to the next table. It is recorded against the table, kept
    out of the CHECK rules and out of the room's counts, and written to the
    CSV — a table you could not reach is not a table fault.
